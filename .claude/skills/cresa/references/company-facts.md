## Structure & ownership

- **Cresa, LLC** — privately held, **100% employee/advisor-owned**, structured like a professional-services consulting firm; equity held by professionals in each office. Managed by corporate staff and a **Board of Directors**.
- Advisor-owned = "we're only incentivized to deliver the best for you, no matter what." Reinforces the conflict-free model.

## History / timeline (highlights)

- **1993** — Founded as an alliance of five commercial real estate firms serving tenants exclusively.
- **1995–1997** — Expands to 30, then 40 offices; Project Management services added (1997).
- **2000** — Lease Administration, Site Selection, and Capital Markets services added.
- **2008** — 50 offices; largest occupier-only CRE firm in North America.
- **2013** — 20-year anniversary; **Cresa Cares** (501c3) formed.
- **2014 / 2015** — First EMEA office (London) / first APAC office (Hong Kong).
- **2017** — Merges ownership of ~20 offices into a single entity (became Cresa Global, Inc.).
- **2018** — Diversity, Inclusion & Belonging (DIB) program / Diversity Council launched.
- **2021** — **Knight Frank partnership** (international advisory across 300+ cities); Tod Lickerman joins as CEO.
- **2022** — Greg Schementi joins as President; acquires **esrp** (Dallas); new **Chicago HQ** opens.
- **2023** — Acquires **Vicus Partners** (NY) and **Axiom Advisory Group** (Detroit).
- **2024** — Acquires **PPM** (doubles Project Management team; adds Transition & Relocation Management), **Fritsche Anderson** (Houston), **NorthStar Owners Representation** (Philadelphia); entity converts to **Cresa, LLC**.

Entity name history (only if specifically asked): Cresa Partners, LLC → re-formed 2012 → **Cresa Global, Inc.** (2017) → **Cresa, LLC** (2024). The founding alliance was originally "Cresa Alliance" (1993); "Cresa Partners LLC" established 1998.

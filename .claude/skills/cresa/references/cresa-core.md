- The platform is **Cresa Core** — use the trademark **Cresa Core™** on first prominent use.
- Tagline lockup: **"Powered by CresaAI | Built on OpenAI."**
- Refer to a capability as **"Core <Module>"** — e.g., *Core Site Selection*, *Core Lease Administration & Lease Accounting*.

## What it is (positioning)

Cresa Core™ is an **occupier-focused intelligence platform** that brings strategy, site selection, lease administration, transaction management, project management, business intelligence, and portfolio optimization together in **one connected ecosystem**.

Powered by **CresaAI**, Core transforms fragmented portfolio, market, labor, and financial data into meaningful intelligence — helping organizations evaluate options, model scenarios, and decide with greater confidence. Unlike traditional software, Core is **continuously refined by experienced real estate professionals who use it every day**. It's a **decision-support platform**, more than a technology solution: built to help occupiers navigate complexity, uncover opportunity, and act with confidence.

Positioning line: *"Cresa Core reflects how occupiers actually make decisions — not how software companies think they should."*

## The six integrated modules

Organizations can adopt a **single module** to solve an immediate challenge or the **full platform** for enterprise-wide strategy.

### 1. Core Lease Administration & Lease Accounting
*Improved accuracy, AI efficiency, and confidence in financial reporting. Compliance, payments & audit-ready controls.*
- AI lease abstraction — up to **80%** efficiency gain
- Critical-date alerts via automated email
- Journal entries with catch-up & reversals
- **ASC 842 / IFRS 16 & GASB 87/96** compliance
- Impairment, remeasurement & modification calcs
- Financial solutions for rent, OpEx, escalations/CAM, and expense reconciliation
- Ad hoc + customizable report library; email integration for audit trail; in-app AI to query leases/portfolio

### 2. Core Business Intelligence
*Anticipate issues, identify opportunities, and make proactive decisions. Dashboards, KPIs & geospatial analytics.*
- Custom dashboards aligned to strategic goals
- Data aggregation from multiple systems via APIs (and Excel import)
- Interactive mapping — color-by, size-by, drive-time
- KPI alerts & trend tracking on any data point
- Rent forecasting and M&A analysis; interactive presentations with live maps

### 3. Core Transaction Management
*Greater visibility, faster execution, and stronger control over transaction outcomes. Full-lifecycle tracking & financial modeling.*
- Auto-kicks transactions from lease critical dates
- Configurable templates for repeatable processes
- Deliverable/milestone tracking with email notifications and original/planned/actual dates
- **AI market briefs** — market rates, recent transactions, competitors, submarket insight
- Portfolio impact reporting — SF & rent changes before/during/after a transaction
- Client-tailored needs-assessment forms

### 4. Core Project Management
*Projects stay on schedule, within budget, and aligned with business priorities. Budget, task & capital planning tools.*
- Client-configurable project templates (repeatable approach, any size/budget)
- Tasks by department; critical-milestone tracking with email reminders
- Program-level funding allocation across projects
- Tailored project budgets for funding approval
- PO & invoice roll-ups for budget control (spot expense leakage)
- Contract/change-order/supplemental tracking; cash-flow forecasting with distribution curves
- Mass update tools for large portfolios

### 5. Core Site Selection
*Choose locations with greater confidence and clear, measurable criteria. Location scoring & comparison tools.*
- Interactive mapping — demographics, labor, customers, competitors, transportation, amenities, zoning, incentives
- Configurable ranking models with weighted criteria
- Drive-time & commute analysis across multiple time bands
- Side-by-side site comparison dashboards (strengths, risks, costs, tradeoffs)
- Scenario planning — adjust weights, toggle layers, test priorities

### 6. Core Labor Analytics
*Align real estate decisions with workforce strategy and long-term growth. 200M+ data points for workforce intel.*
- Analyzes labor availability, talent concentration, employment trends, hiring competitiveness
- Wage levels, salary cost impact, predicted compensation changes by location
- Employee commute, transit access, proximity to population centers
- Surrounding amenities, housing, education, quality-of-life factors
- Forecasts future workforce growth; evaluates any U.S. market to street level; AI-generated insights
